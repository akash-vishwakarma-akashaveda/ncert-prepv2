## What changed

### Home page (`pages/LandingPage.tsx`)
- Back to the indigo-hero design you chose; the experimental "professional" redesign is saved on branch
  `backup/design-2026-10-05-landing-pre-pro` (local).
- Hero counters now include **registered students** (exact count from the users table).
- "One app. Three looks" became **"Every class from 1 to 12, in one place."**: new copy, Primary / Middle / Senior
  labels, buttons count lessons across each whole class range (155 / 288 / 457).
- Subject tapes now divide the blue hero from the cream section (no leftover cream gap).
- Wavy section edges are cut into the lower section (`.wave-top`), so doodles and dots run into the curve.
- "Notes" wording replaced with **NCERT books**; FAQ says "India's data protection law".
- "Pick your class": empty "Start your first lesson" box only shows when there is a lesson to resume.

### Class buttons
- Roman numerals redrawn as puffy 3D letters in a white badge (`ClassGrid.tsx`, `.numeral-3d`), used on the home page,
  demo, onboarding and profile.

### Try demo (`pages/DemoPage.tsx`)
- Indigo confetti hero, doodle bands, sticker headings and the indigo closing banner, matching the home page.

### Lesson page (`student/pages/LessonPage.tsx`)
- Title, Save, Mark complete and Next sit directly under the video on a white card; subscribe banner after them.
- Sidebar is a thumbnail playlist with progress bar, Now playing, locks and ticks.
- **Chapters with several lectures**: chapter header with "2 lectures · 1 done", lectures listed underneath,
  "Lecture 1 of 2" in the header, "Next lecture" / "Next chapter" button.
- Page background follows the class (cream for 1–5, graph paper for 6–10, plain for 11–12).
- Notes tab removed (the Overview tab links the NCERT textbook PDF).

### Subjects and textbooks
- **Every class** now uses the Class 1–5 flow: big subject cards, then a winding chapter path. Plainer wording from
  Class 6 up, "Book" signposts for subjects with several books, Class 11–12 streams kept.
- **Textbooks** rebuilt: subject buttons, one card per book with numbered chapters and a Read button; title-case
  for ALL-CAPS chapter names. Class 11–12 show only the chosen stream's subjects (plus English, Hindi, PE).
- Both pages use the full width.

### Navigation, subscribe button, scrolling
- Every new page opens at the top (Back/Forward and #section links excepted).
- Subscribe button: in the app's top bar; bottom-right corner on public pages; hidden on lesson pages
  (they have the banner under the video).

### Referral goodies (new)
- Admin: **Dashboard Control → Referral Goodies**: on/off switch, banner message, how-to-claim line, live preview.
- When on, a yellow scrolling banner sits at the top of every public and student page, with "Get your code"
  (visitors) or "My referral code" (students → Profile → Referrals). Off by default.
- Profile → Referrals shows "Referral you joined with" for the screenshot.

### Profile
- Optional **City** field: last onboarding step and Profile settings (new `city` column).

### Footer and stats
- Footer shows the **visitor count** (each browser counted once a day, so it is daily visits).
- New public endpoint `GET /api/stats/public` → `{ visitors, students }`, cached for a minute.

### Readability
- Lesson title block on a card; breadcrumb in a pill; a script checked public pages for text sitting directly on
  the doodle wallpaper and found none after the fixes.
