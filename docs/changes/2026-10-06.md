## Ready, not yet deployed

### Age rules at sign-up (owner's decision, 6 Oct)
- **Under 13**: parent's name, email and phone are required (as before), parent gets the confirm email.
- **13 to 17**: same as 18+: tick the consent box and continue. No parent details. The age band is still recorded
  as `13-17`.
- Backend: the parent-details route now accepts Under 13 only; the self-consent route accepts 13–17 and 18+.
- Existing accounts are unchanged.

Note: under India's DPDP Act 2023, parental consent applies to everyone under 18. Letting 13–17 consent for
themselves is the owner's decision; worth confirming with your legal adviser before this goes live.

## Answers given today
- **Do class lesson counts update when the sheet changes?** The counts are computed live from the lesson database.
  The backend re-reads a linked Google Sheet every 10 minutes, but production has **no sheet linked**. Link it once in
  Admin → Data & Sync and counts follow the sheet within about 10 minutes.
